Accessibility Statement

Our approach to creating a more usable and inclusive portfolio experience.

Maison Rivière is a fictional portfolio concept. This statement describes the accessibility goals of the demonstration website.

Our accessibility goal

This portfolio project is intended to provide a usable experience for as many visitors as practical, including people who navigate by keyboard, use assistive technology, enlarge text, or prefer reduced motion.

Standards and approach

The project is designed toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA best practices where practical. This statement is not a guarantee of legal compliance and does not replace ongoing testing.

Current accessibility features

The site includes responsive layouts, visible keyboard focus states, reduced-motion support, text alternatives for many images, semantic headings, and browser-native form controls.

Known areas for improvement

Some interactive components in this portfolio demonstration may still require additional keyboard semantics, labeling, focus management, or testing with screen readers. Gallery image descriptions and form label associations may also be refined further.

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility should be treated as an ongoing part of design, development, content updates and quality assurance.

Feedback

Because Maison Rivière is a fictional venue concept, this demonstration does not provide a venue accessibility office or customer-support channel.
