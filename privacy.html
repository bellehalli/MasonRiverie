Privacy Policy

How information is handled within this fictional portfolio website.

Maison Rivière is a fictional venue concept created as a web design portfolio demonstration. It is not an operating wedding venue.

About this website

This website demonstrates the design, functionality, lead qualification, planning tools and digital experience that could be created for a luxury hospitality or wedding venue.

Information you enter

The inquiry form displayed on this website is a portfolio demonstration. Information entered into the form is not submitted to a real Maison Rivière venue, booking department or sales team.

The current demonstration does not intentionally transmit or permanently store the information entered into that form.

Wedding Builder

The Wedding Builder processes selections within your browser to display a sample estimated investment. These calculations are for demonstration purposes only and do not constitute a quote, contract or reservation.

Cookies and tracking

The portfolio website itself does not intentionally use advertising trackers, marketing pixels or analytics cookies.

The hosting platform may process standard technical information necessary to serve and protect the website, such as request information, device or browser details, and network information, according to the hosting provider's own practices.

Payments and reservations

This website does not process payments, accept deposits, create legally binding reservations or sell wedding services.

Third-party services

If third-party services, analytics, booking systems, payment processors, email platforms or other integrations are added to a future version of this project, this policy should be updated to accurately describe those services and their data practices.

Changes to this policy

This privacy information may be updated if the functionality of the portfolio website changes.
